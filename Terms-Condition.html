Dazzling Gown: Admin & Employee Operational Terms

Effective Date: April 21, 2026

This portal is an internal tool for managing the inventory and rentals of Dazzling Gown. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

1. Data Privacy Compliance (RA 10173)

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), all employees must handle customer information with absolute confidentiality.

  • Unauthorized processing, access, or disclosure of customer data is strictly prohibited.
  • Data must only be used for the legitimate purpose of rental management.

2. Cybercrime Prevention (RA 10175)

Under the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10175), any attempt at:

  • Illegal Access: Entering the system without authority.
  • Data Interference: Intentional alteration or deletion of inventory records without right.
...will be subject to criminal prosecution and immediate termination.

3. Consumer Protection Standards (RA 7394)

To uphold the Consumer Act of the Philippines (Republic Act No. 7394), employees must ensure that all rental descriptions, prices, and gown conditions recorded in the system are truthful and non-deceptive to protect our clients in Tondo.

4. Professional Conduct

Users are responsible for all actions performed under their account. Audit logs are maintained to track changes in the "Dazzle-Track" system.
